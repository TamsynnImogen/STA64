<https://github.com/gradle/gradle/tree/v8.13.0>. Gradle's Apache License 2.0
is retained in `GRADLE-LICENSE.txt`. The wrapper JAR SHA-256 is

recorded in `gradle/wrapper/gradle-wrapper.properties`.
